# Privacy Policy — Candle

_Last updated: 30 August 2026_

**This app collects nothing.**

Not "collects only what it needs", not "collects anonymised usage data". It
has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no account, and no
server to send anything to. It does not declare the `INTERNET` permission, so
it is not technically capable of transmitting data off your device even if it
tried to.

## Permissions

**Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`)** — optional, and only requested at the moment
you tap the microphone button. It is used for one thing: detecting the rush of
air when you blow at the phone, so the flame goes out.

The audio is analysed as it arrives, entirely on your device, to measure the
energy in the low-frequency band that a puff of breath produces. It is never
recorded, never written to storage, and never transmitted. Turning the feature
off releases the microphone immediately.

The app works completely without this permission. Declining it costs you the
blow-out gesture and nothing else.

**Vibration (`VIBRATE`)** — for the small haptic ticks as you turn the dial.
This permission is granted automatically by Android and gives no access to any
information about you.

## Data the app stores

None. The candle's state is not written to disk; closing the app discards it.

## Children

The app contains no ads, no purchases, no user-generated content and no
communication features of any kind.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on the project's
